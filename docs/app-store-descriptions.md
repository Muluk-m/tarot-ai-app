## iOS App Store Description

Unlock the ancient wisdom of tarot with Celestial Eye — where mystical tradition meets modern AI technology. Whether you're seeking daily guidance or want to master the art of tarot reading, this beautifully crafted app is your gateway to self-discovery.

**AI-POWERED TAROT READINGS**

• Daily Card Draw — Get quick guidance for your day with a single card reading
• Three-Card Spread — Explore your past, present, and future with deeper insights
• Personalized Interpretations — Advanced AI analyzes your cards to deliver meaningful, context-aware guidance
• Beautiful Animations — Experience immersive shuffle and card reveal animations
• Reading History — Save and revisit your past readings anytime

**LEARN TAROT LIKE A PRO**

• Interactive Flashcards — Master all 78 cards with our spaced repetition system
• Comprehensive Courses — Structured lessons from beginner to advanced
• Tarot Encyclopedia — Detailed meanings for Major and Minor Arcana
• Knowledge Quizzes — Test your understanding and track your progress
• Personal Journal — Document your readings and spiritual journey
• Achievements — Earn badges as you advance in your tarot mastery

**WHY CELESTIAL EYE?**

• Complete 78-card Rider-Waite inspired deck
• Stunning mystical design with gold and purple aesthetics
• No ads or interruptions
• Works offline — your data stays on your device
• Privacy-focused — no account required, no data collection

Begin your mystical journey today. Draw your first card and discover what the universe has in store for you.
